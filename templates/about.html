About TruthGuard

TruthGuard is an AI-powered fake news detection system built with Flask and Scikit-learn. It uses Natural Language Processing (TF-IDF) combined with classical machine learning classifiers to estimate whether a piece of news text is likely Real or Fake, based on patterns learned from a labeled training dataset.

Disclaimer: TruthGuard is an educational project. Its predictions reflect statistical patterns in its training data and should never be treated as a definitive fact-check. Always verify important news through trusted, independent sources.
